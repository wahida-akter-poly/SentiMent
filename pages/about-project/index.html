SENTI-MIND

One intelligent view of how people feel across the digital world.

01

Project Overview

SENTI-MIND is designed to collect, organize, and interpret opinions expressed on digital platforms. The system will present clear sentiment insights through an accessible dashboard.

02

Problem Statement

Digital feedback is high-volume, unstructured, and spread across many channels. Manually understanding public opinion is slow, inconsistent, and difficult to scale.

03

Proposed Solution

A text analysis platform that classifies feedback as positive, neutral, negative, or derived mixed sentiment and visualizes persisted patterns over time.

04

Objectives

  • Analyze audience sentiment accurately
  • Compare sentiment across platforms
  • Visualize trends and actionable insights
  • Support data-driven decisions
